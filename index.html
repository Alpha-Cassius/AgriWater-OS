How this Dashboard Helps You

Welcome to AgriWater OS. This tool acts as a "Digital Twin" of your farm, enabling precision agriculture without expensive hardware.

  • Precision Irrigation: Avoid overwatering or crop stress by tracking exact water deficit (ET₀ - P).
  • Pollination Tracking: Monitor exact atmospheric conditions to optimize periods where bees and butterflies will naturally pollinate your crops.
  • Future-Proofing: Discover which cash crops will thrive on your land using historical data, rather than guessing.

The Kaggle KNN ML Engine

Instead of simple rules, our dashboard uses a production-ready Machine Learning algorithm called KNN, trained dynamically on the 2,200 row Kaggle Crop Recommendation dataset. It plots your farm's data across 7 dimensions (N,P,K,Temp,Humidity,pH,Rainfall) to calculate statistical matches.

Data Sources & APIs (Production Ready)

Open-Meteo API

We rely on Open-Meteo for real-time forecasting and historical archive data. It aggregates models from national weather services like NOAA (USA) and DWD (Germany). It is highly reliable, updates hourly, and provides the critical Evapotranspiration (ET₀) and Soil Moisture metrics used in our irrigation planner.

Geochemical Modeling (LST & NPK)

Because live NPK (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium) APIs are extremely slow, our engine uses a Weathering Proxy Model. It estimates soil texture and nutrient retention capacity based on historical leaching and thermal breakdown.
